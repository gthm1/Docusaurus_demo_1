# Internal Pricing Notes

Staff-only reference for standard vs. negotiated pricing tiers across AlphaX Cloud, AlphaX XVision, and Xmesh LoRaWAN. Never shared externally.

## Standard tiers

- AlphaX Cloud: Starter / Growth / Enterprise
- XVision: per-camera-stream licensing
- Xmesh: per-gateway licensing, volume discounts above 50 units

Check the internal CRM for any customer-specific pricing overrides before quoting.
